# QRC: five-direction assessment — reference manifest

Search cutoff: 2026-09-26. Targeted narrative assessment, not an exhaustive systematic review. Publisher articles, arXiv version records, OpenAlex/PubMed metadata and Crossref were used. Selection prioritizes direct overlap with the five proposed directions, rather than journal prestige or favorable results. Abstracts and relevant methods/results were inspected; no independent replication or proof audit was performed.
